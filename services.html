Choosing a treatment

Find a massage style that feels right

Different massage styles offer different experiences. Think about the kind of session you prefer, then speak with the team about pressure, comfort, session length and availability before booking.

  • Relaxation massage: a gentler, flowing option for a slower-paced experience. Explore relaxation massage.
  • Swedish massage: a classic style using smooth, flowing strokes, with pressure discussed before the session. Explore Swedish massage.
  • Aromatherapy massage: combines massage with aromatic oils; ask which blends are available and mention fragrance sensitivities. Explore aromatherapy.
  • Deep tissue and hot stone: focused pressure or warming stones may suit different preferences; discuss comfort and suitability with the team first. Deep tissue · Hot stone.
  • Focused sessions: enquire about foot relaxation or head, neck and shoulder massage. Foot relaxation · Head, neck & shoulder.
